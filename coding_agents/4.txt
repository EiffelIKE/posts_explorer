  Choices I made that you may want to change
  - History: typing replaces the current history entry rather than adding one
    per keystroke, and the scroll position stays put. Otherwise Back would undo
    one letter at a time, and on mobile the list would jump to the top on every
    keystroke.

    consistency. If you'd rather it reset the search, it's a one-line change in
    RootLayout.tsx (src/layout/RootLayout.tsx).

    when read, to match maxLength.

    affects it until step 5.
