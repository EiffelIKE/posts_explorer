     - A failed publish shows the reason and keeps the draft.

     (src/posts/UserPostsPanel/loadErrors.test.tsx) covers /users and
     /users/:id/posts.

     - Opening a user while /users is failing shows the error in both panels.
     - While a retry runs, the error stays visible and focus stays on the
       "Retrying…" button.

     (src/posts/UserPostsPanel/userSwitch.test.tsx):

       the post shows up on Leanne's panel.

       to them. After a simulated reload (a fresh query cache) it's gone.
